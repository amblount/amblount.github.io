#### what does it mean to be an entrpreneur in residence 
The kapor center is a venture capital firm in Oakalnd, CA and like any other VC they provode venture capital for startups all around the country mostly tech startups. As a part of a new venture - the ilab they provide office space inside of their really nice building downtown oakland and mentorship. The idea is to support under represented founders and help the, get their ideas off of the ground. They dpntrpvode any funding its just office space. The idea is cool but not practical. The cost of livign is high and there is no way honestly to build a startup with no money especially as an under represented founder. Having wifi and office space is great if you have seed funding coming from somewhere else. During this period of time I was working as a data science intern with Acorns to fund my cost of livivn while trying to build a startup on the side. The idea was cool and I think with more resources it could have turned into something.
